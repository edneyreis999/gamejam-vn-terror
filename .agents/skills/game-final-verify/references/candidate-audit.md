# Candidate Inclusion Audit

This is a read-only inclusion review of every proposed delivery artifact, updated after scoped edits. It does not introduce separate document/environment purge lenses or authorize archival operations.

For each file, or homogeneous group with the same purpose and consumer, identify:

- Its maintained consumer or unique decision/evidence.
- What omission would lose, and any maintained source already preserving that value.
- Duplicated authority, temporary assumptions or ongoing maintenance burden.
- For deletions, surviving consumers and unique knowledge at risk.

Assign `keep | fix | consolidate | archive | defer` with a concrete reason and destination, and explicitly include or exclude each candidate from the claimed ready set. A destination is a proposal unless applying it is within the user's authorization. Preserve unrelated pending work. Counts of additions, modifications and deletions account for scope; they are not reduction targets.

Source, tests, data, baselines and consumed generated files need maintained or reproducible inputs. Delivered behavior, decisions, results and limitations belong in existing authoritative documentation. Raw logs or extra captures may remain local evidence once the necessary facts are preserved; local availability is not a fresh-clone guarantee or long-term backup. Do not delete useful history because it is old, generated or voluminous.

Read the result as a future maintainer from a fresh clone of the selected candidate: required links resolve to tracked/candidate inputs or documented generation; current instructions do not depend on the conversation, ignored state or absolute machine paths. A disposable reconstruction is warranted only to resolve actual uncertainty. Historical comparisons may link explicitly labeled local archives and need not be executable from the final tree; do not present them as reusable current procedures.

Read status-bearing files together, preserving dated historical facts while keeping live status under one owner. An unresolved fix cannot be silently excluded if the remaining claimed delivery depends on it. Conclude with justified inclusion/exclusion for every candidate, remaining corrections and the precise boundary of candidate readiness, separate from product verification and Git publication.
