# Prologue presentation

Owns RQ-003 and RQ-004. Follow [script](prologo-rheed.narrativa.md) N01–N06. Black during older Rheed’s narration and the existing tavern during Ivaí/young Rheed’s direct dialogue, with no audio, are prototype baselines, not a new art/audio direction for the entire game.

Use the existing lower message window and native reading controls. Older Rheed owns N01–N03, Ivaí N04 and N06; N04–N06 show young Rheed on the left and Ivaí on the right; N05 identifies Rheed as the speaker. Never substitute older Rheed's image for the young speaker. No new scene illustrations or document inserts, hero reactions, voice acting, music, ambience or authored sound effects.

Keep text readable without clipping and busts clear of the text. N01–N03 show only older Rheed; N04–N06 show both interlocutors. Active speaker height is 480 native pixels and listening height is 480/1.1, bases aligned above the dialogue; emphasis switches instantly with the line. Player acknowledgement of N06 transfers to existing preparation immediately, without an added pause or interpretation. Clear outgoing pictures and attachments. Preserve HIDE, Settings, FAST's seen-text-only policy, keyboard/mouse navigation and reduced motion. Use the supported native QA viewports; native browser zoom testing remains excluded by ADR-G003.

Silence applies to prologue playback; do not mute global preferences or silence subsequent preparation. Human review judges whether the opening is brief and its subtext sufficiently open; no automated test proves that effect.


User scope clarification: final fitting of portraits against the dialogue box and a revised backdrop for older Rheed are deferred beyond this update. Current placement and black narration background remain the delivered baseline; see canonical GDD section 27.4.
