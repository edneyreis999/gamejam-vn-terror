# ADR-001 — Rheed narrates the revised prologue

Status: accepted product decisions; consolidated spec set approved by the user on 2026-09-16.

## Context and decision

The user completed a one-question-at-a-time interview and authorized consolidating the decisions and publishing a local GDD 5.0 snapshot. The increment replaces only the opening with [the accepted script](../prologo-rheed.narrativa.md), colloquial older-Rheed narration, occasional direct Ivaí speech and a final publicly audible question by young Rheed. No hero speaks or reacts. The opening ends with Ivaí's exact answer.

Rheed distributed the announcements, serves Ivaí, knows the drowning curse and accompanies expeditions as a non-player-controlled witness. He consumes no party slot, solves no traps and cannot be sacrificed; he can survive the all-hero/Ivaí death ending to tell the story. This is accepted fictional framing, not a request to modify campaign-wide scenes in this delivery.

The user explicitly retained the introductory wording “a contragosto de Irati” and Ivaí's insistence against the warning despite the identified chronology and trust risks. Do not rewrite it or infer a changed date of Irati's death. The wording is an accepted ambiguity. Initial trust/curiosity remains an artistic target, not a guaranteed audience response.

## Consequences and deferred work

Specific tradition content is outside scope and must not appear as a playable placeholder. Young-Rheed artwork is a future deliverable; only older art's actual location remains a current dependency. Other scenes do not receive Rheed narration now. No engine/vendor code changes, remote assets or new mechanics are approved.

The [GDD §27](../../../../docs/GDD_Visual_Novel_Expedicao_e_Sacrificio.md#27-prólogo-de-rheed--gdd-50) records these replacements in canonical authority; v4.0 remains immutable. GDD v5.0 is a snapshot of the updated canonical document, not proof of implementation.
