# ADR-003 — Approved prose replacements and whole-image epilogues

Status: accepted product and surface decisions, D-001–003/006/007/018. The complete spec set was approved on 2026-09-18; implementation remains pending.

## Decision

PR #15's supplied epilogue prose and eight illustrations replace the corresponding earlier hero-sheet epilogues and native bust presentation. Each eligible hero receives one fixed source epilogue, independent of the medallion choice. Fit its complete illustration proportionally within the game area, centered over black; retain native lower narration and reading controls, with no separate bust. The user selected whole-image fit over edge cropping on 2026-09-18.

PR #16 supplies the first/second route closings, replacement Council revelation and three ending texts. Completion order selects the initial-route closing after the lover and map-piece sequence. Its Council revelation replaces the coffer discovery through confession; continue with Andirá, eligible opinions, the final Irati excerpt and the choice. Existing ending images remain; the additional illustrations described by the source are not deliverables.

The runtime wording and approved source art are not reopened for editorial acceptance. Native segmentation preserves the supplied words and order. Source headings and illustration directions do not become dialogue automatically. D-008 defers solo variants without altering the actual present participants. D-017 separately governs trap prose in [ADR-002](adr-002-trap-prose-integration-boundary.md).

## Supersession and preserved rules

This replaces the canonical GDD's literal authority of earlier hero-sheet epilogue wording and its previous epilogue-bust rule. The [canonical GDD](../../../../docs/GDD_Visual_Novel_Expedicao_e_Sacrificio.md) now names the approved replacement and links this record. Completed specs remain historical.

Do not create a gallery, additional ending choices, new rewards, new outcome art or a narrator bust over illustrated epilogues/endings.

## Consequences

Seven 3:2 epilogue images show black side margins on the 16:9 logical game area. Elowen's approximately 16:9 image nearly fills it. This preserves the supplied composition without stretching, cropping or invented background extensions. HIDE allows viewing the image without the lower text overlay.

During integration, synchronize the eight hero sheets' epilogue sections with the accepted PR #15 prose and link the source revision; retain unrelated sheet fields. Those editorial synchronization writes are scoped implementation work, not a request for fresh prose. Technical verification covers source fidelity, association, eligibility, readable native rendering and lifecycle. It does not require the user to resolve deferred creative refinements.
