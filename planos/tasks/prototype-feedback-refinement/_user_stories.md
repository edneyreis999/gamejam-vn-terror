# Player stories — Prototype feedback refinement

Approved product scope for [spec.md](spec.md), accepted under D-016. The requirement IDs and verification ownership remain in the spec and [verification.md](verification.md); this file does not introduce additional requirements. Surface/technical design and implementation remain separate from this product approval.

| Story | As a player, I want… | So that… | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US-001 | A recognizable title menu followed by a dedicated age notice on each new-game or Continue entry | I understand what I am entering and acknowledge the notice deliberately | RQ-001 |
| US-002 | To form my party, choose a route on a map, then confirm departure | I understand the preparation order and can revise choices before leaving | RQ-002 |
| US-003 | Rheed to welcome me and introduce Ivaí and Irati without revealing Ivaí's possible fate | I can follow the story and discover its consequences through play | RQ-003 |
| US-004 | Portraits framed proportionally with the dialogue box | I can see the speaker and read comfortably | RQ-004 |
| US-005 | A tavern noticeboard listing only deceased heroes' names, without additional descriptive text or images, with a clear empty state | I can revisit losses in a form that belongs to the setting | RQ-005; D-020 refinement |
| US-006 | Each hero's portrait and aligned name to share one complete interaction target | I can open the intended hero reliably with mouse or keyboard | RQ-006 |
| US-007 | Revised hero dialogue and an automatic return after acknowledging a successful selection | I hear the character and continue preparation without an extra exit action | RQ-007 |
| US-008 | Consequences to name the hero I actually chose after their farewell | The narrative reflects my irreversible decision | RQ-008 |
| US-009 | Narrative choice buttons to use a consistent visual language | I recognize available actions and focus states across scenes | RQ-009 |
| US-010 | Rheed to narrate the existing eligible epilogues over black with the present-day music and discreet audience, without opening applause | The closing presentation follows the narrator's established form | RQ-010 |
| US-011 | A brief visual transition after route completion | Returning to the tavern feels deliberate without adding dialogue | RQ-011 |
| US-012 | Each sacrifice candidate's entire card to be selectable | The displayed target matches the irreversible action I intend | RQ-012 |
| US-013 | All deceased heroes to disappear together for three seconds after expedition returns | I perceive the accumulated losses before preparing again | RQ-013 |
| US-014 | The two final medallion choices to be prominent and equally usable | I recognize this decision and choose deliberately | RQ-014 |
| US-015 | The complete cemetery inscriptions to fit legibly | I can read every deceased hero's intended entry | RQ-015 |
| US-016 | Visible settings and a command to save the current campaign in its own file | I can adjust preferences or save preparation without creating rollback slots | RQ-016 |

Preserve reduced-party progression, permanent deaths, route unlocks, hidden competencies, finale consequences, survivor eligibility and player-controlled reading. Reduced motion shows empty tavern places immediately. No gamepad or native-browser-zoom testing is added. Runtime implementation and task decomposition are outside this authoring stage.
