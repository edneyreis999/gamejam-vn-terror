# ADR001 — Event-owned bust presentation

Status: accepted by the user's implementation request on 2026-09-11.

The artist authors visual validation and focus in native VNPictureBusts commands. EventBridge retains restoration and conversation lifecycle, plus its unrelated campaign responsibilities. Its visual parameter bounds and focus policy are removed. This explicitly replaces the previous GDD restrictions on enforced original orientation and global-only focus settings for this increment; the initial migration preserves existing art and direction.

Restoration delegates native visual commands to the vendor, rebuilding the final composition at the reading boundary. No replay of narrative or campaign actions is allowed. Replaying visual expressions uses current game state; author expressions must have no side effects. One-shot battle animations do not repeat, and continuous cycles may restart. A frame-exact animation snapshot system is outside this increment.
