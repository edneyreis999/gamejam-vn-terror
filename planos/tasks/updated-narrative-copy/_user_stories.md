# Player stories

| Story | Player outcome | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| As a player meeting a hero, I want a direct conversation so I can infer their motivation and abilities from what they say. | Ivaí starts the source dialogue; no recited profile; all eight heroes are covered. | RQ-004, RQ-008 |
| As a player forming an expedition, I want the hero's response to match whether I added them or the party is full. | Correct source reply without accidental selection or replacement. | RQ-005 |
| As a player facing a trap, I want the description, approach and result to tell the same event. | All sixteen encounters use the mapped source copy; reread is consistent. | RQ-001–003 |
| As a player sacrificing a companion, I want to choose before reading their farewell and death. | One committed death, appropriate farewell, then contextual narration. | RQ-003, RQ-006 |
| As a player at the Council, I want the surviving witnesses to react and give their advice. | Both opinion paragraphs, correct speakers and existing eligibility. | RQ-006 |
| As a player reading the memorial, I want each inscription to agree with the death I witnessed. | Approved A1/A5 inscriptions and saved public locations. | RQ-007 |
| As a returning player, I want to finish pending reading without repeating my decision. | Candidate-earned Continue preserves committed actions and reading boundaries. | RQ-008 |

Acceptance and evidence live in [spec.md](spec.md) and [verification.md](verification.md). Source approval does not assert the integrated game has been tested.
