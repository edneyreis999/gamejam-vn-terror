# Player stories

- As a player, I can pause after two dangers to spend time with the surviving party before choosing whether to continue. RQ-001/002.
- As a player, I can hear everyone, one person or nobody, without a mandatory dialogue quota. RQ-003.
- As a player, I discover habits and hopes that make each hero more than a useful set of abilities. RQ-003/012.
- As a player, I see that earlier losses still matter when a replacement party reaches the fire. RQ-004/005.
- As a player, I hear a survivor remember a companion even if they were not on the same expedition. RQ-006/007.
- As a player, I can recognize a new subject without reopening every hero's menu. RQ-008.
- As a returning player, I retain read subjects and completed grief reactions in my campaign save. RQ-011.
- As a keyboard user or a player using reduced motion, I can access the same subjects and understand who is absent. RQ-009.
