# Narrative contract and complete bilingual writing proposal

Owns RQ-003/004/006/007/012 in [spec.md](spec.md). This file contains proposed text, not approved new canon or a runtime generator input. Existing hero sheets and current Languages.tsv establish voices/motivations; any new small anecdote below is an editorial proposal. No named new relative, destination discovery or ending revelation is required by this writing.

## Reading the proposal

Each scene ID is stable; numbered rows define proposed localization suffixes `.01`, `.02`, etc. Speaker names are metadata, not prefixes pasted into the message body. `Rheed` means young Rheed; `Older Rheed` means the colored narrator. All prose is literal PT-BR/EN-US. Rows in base scenes play in order. Interjections are appended only at the specified base scene with the named companion living and present; the base scene remains complete without them.

Church/Park scenes work in either route order and avoid lover/map discoveries not yet earned. Village scenes may acknowledge approaching the final destination but do not explain the medallion, Ivaí's concealed plan or final alternatives. A/B scenes stand alone. Humor is restrained and does not celebrate a death-free party after losses.

After approval and integration, Languages.tsv and native events own the current text/sequence. Keep this draft as historical review material; do not enforce ongoing equality through snapshots or document-to-data generators.
