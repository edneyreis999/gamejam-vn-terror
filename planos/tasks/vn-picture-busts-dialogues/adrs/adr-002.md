# ADR-002 — Interview direction for native ensemble dialogue staging

- Date: 2026-09-11
- Status: accepted interview decisions; the later execution authorization is recorded in [ADR-003](adr-003.md)
- Scope: product decisions made during the interview; the original pending approval checkpoints below were superseded by ADR-003

## Accepted direction

The user wants to edit bust selection and positioning manually through VNPictureBusts commands in the RPG Maker MZ editor. Existing artwork and dialogue remain the starting point.

A character enters when their speaking participation begins and remains while the other participants speak. Participants leave together at the end of the conversation. A one-person passage does not acquire a second character merely to fill a slot.

The user accepted treating the tavern's public hero profile and subsequent Ivaí/hero dialogue as one visual conversation. Keep the hero's bust through the profile-to-dialogue boundary; Ivaí enters on his first line. Both leave when that conversation finishes. Existing passage identities, text order and formation rules remain unchanged.

Heroes occupy the left side. Ivaí and other non-hero characters occupy the right side, subject to resolving established scene-specific compositions. The user withdrew the earlier request for characters to face one another: preserve the original artwork orientation instead of introducing mirroring.

For ensemble scenes, including the Council discussion, the user wants the eligible participants retained in separate positions instead of replacing each hero with the next speaker. They accepted movement and increased size as a means of identifying the speaker. They subsequently accepted making listeners slightly smaller and dimming them softly, while the speaker retains normal tone and the calibrated active scale. Exact displacement, scale ratio, dimming strength and timing remain to be specified; the reference's global scale and Dark preset were not accepted as literal target values.

The user accepted introducing all eligible Council heroes together at the narrated demand for explanations, before their individual opinions. This is an explicit exception to first-line entry. Their fixed left-side positions persist through the subsequent conversation and are restored after Andirá's intervention. The user also allowed any tavern conversation as the first representative validation sample; Gorvak/Ivaí was selected for its alternating and consecutive speaker boxes.

After requesting analysis of ProjectX Map020 and its Common Events, the user accepted the recommendation to adapt its event-based pattern to the game's own portraits and keep the commands visible/editable in MZ. This selects a reusable authoring pattern, not a new generic plugin, automatic speaker detection, copied ProjectX switches, or a specific helper implementation.

After reviewing the addressee of Andirá's Council line, the user accepted a side exception for that intervention: Andirá appears on the left, opposite Ivaí on the right, while remaining confined to the water reflection. The user subsequently accepted temporarily hiding the heroes' busts during this intervention and bringing them back for their opinions after Andirá exits. This is a presentation change, not a narrative departure, party change or eligibility change. Ivaí remains on the right through the transition.

## Reference findings

The reference connects four focus switches to four parallel Common Events. Each restores the active picture's global scale and normal tone, reduces the others to 90% with a dark tone, then disables its switch. Its focus effect does not change picture draw order or translate the speaker. The same VNPictureBusts plugin file is installed in both projects.

See [reference-map020-analysis.md](../reference-map020-analysis.md) for exact event locators, source fingerprints, adaptation risks and the proposed delivery strategy. Reference settings are observations, not approved target values.

## Replaced proposal and authority conflict

This direction supersedes the draft proposal to preserve one central, unmirrored bust at a time with no focus transitions. The consolidated spec and discipline/verification companions now describe the ensemble design, with prototype settings and technical details explicitly proposed for complete-set review.

The canonical GDD was updated during consolidation to replace one-bust-at-a-time Council staging with the accepted ensemble/intervention sequence and to record native authorship and tavern continuity. Numerical tuning and unaccepted boundary proposals were not promoted to confirmed GDD rules. Andirá's reflection and the lovers' established prisons remain constraints.
