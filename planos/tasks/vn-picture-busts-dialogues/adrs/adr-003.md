# ADR-003 — Complete tavern coverage and uninterrupted task execution

- Date: 2026-09-11
- Status: accepted user execution direction
- Scope: this incremental spec, its discipline/verification contracts and its complete task loop

## Decision source

After the interview consolidation, the user explicitly requested coverage of all tavern dialogue rather than only Gorvak and Ivaí. They then instructed that execution must not pause for approval of the scope, a conversation or any task: the model must continue the loop through the last task.

This is current authorization to decompose and execute the consolidated increment locally. It supersedes the pending complete-set and per-task approval checkpoints recorded at the end of ADR-002. It does not revive the withdrawn approval in ADR-001 or claim that implementation has already happened.

## Complete tavern scope

Include Gorvak, Elowen, Griznik, Seraphina, Bimbren, Liora, Vaelith and Draska. For each hero, migrate profile, Ivaí/hero conversation, successful-selection feedback and full-party feedback: 32 sections and 72 Show Text commands. These are part of the already inventoried 63 sections / 103 boxes, not additional story content.

Each profile and subsequent conversation share one visual lifetime. Selection and full-party feedback remain one-person lines. System status feedback keeps its existing role; do not invent a character speaker or add Ivaí merely to populate a second slot. Every hero and all four families require coverage. Gorvak may illustrate the devlog, but is neither a separate delivery nor a human approval checkpoint.

## Execution and verification

- The spec and its companions are authorized for execution. Future task graph creation inherits this authorization without another approval round.
- No task may depend on a user/discipline approval, sample acceptance or permission to continue. This includes QA and final verification tasks.
- Treat the documented numerical focus, boundary and motion settings as a delegated prototype baseline. Calibrate within the accepted design, validate the result and record the actual authored values. Do not present those values as earlier individual user choices or final art acceptance.
- Keep required static, automated, integration, directed gameplay and agent visual checks. Fix in-scope failures and continue until all tasks finish, or a concrete technical blocker prevents dependent work. Missing approval is not a blocker.
- Keep human review available as non-blocking refinement. Do not infer that absent reviews occurred or mark human_accepted true. Lack of that review alone cannot block task completion or the local prototype verdict.
- Preserve all unrelated scope boundaries, source integrity, campaign rules and the prohibition on automatic commits or remote publication.

## Consequences

The consolidated contracts no longer await a second complete-set sign-off. Execution readiness is distinct from implementation/runtime verification, which remain unfulfilled until evidence exists. Task authoring must transport complete coverage and technical verification ownership, without adding human gates at the end of the graph.
