# Player and editorial stories

- As an English-speaking player, I can start the game in English and understand
  its full story, interface and choices without needing Portuguese.
- As a Portuguese-speaking player, I can use the familiar language option to
  play in Portuguese and have my preference remembered.
- As a player, I can change language without losing my campaign decisions.
- As a player, I can understand the prose and choices while experiencing the
  intended dread, uncertainty and character voices.
- As Edney, I receive a complete agent-produced translation plus a separate
  agent's player-perspective readability report, and retain editorial approval.

Acceptance requirements and evidence ownership: [spec](spec.md) and
[verification](verification.md).
