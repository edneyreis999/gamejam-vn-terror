## Ivaí

Arquivo: `../personagens/ivai.png`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: single static character portrait, portrait 2:3 composition.
Input image 1: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, the supplied Gorvak drawing. Match its confident irregular black ink outlines, varied line weight, fine hand-drawn hatching and sparse loose sketch marks, with flat warm pale beige fills. Use white or off-white negative space. Shadows come from hatching, not smooth gradients. Same apparent artist and finish level. No painterly rendering, glossy light, photorealism, 3D, saturated colors, captions, lettering, UI, borders, logos or watermarks. This is provisional concept artwork for the PT-BR horror visual novel Afogados em Terra Seca, in a fantasy world with no humans, inspired by inland Paraná, Brazil and late-nineteenth-century material culture.
Primary request: Ivaí, a 30-year-old male fantasy dwarf, an itinerant bard, singer and chronicler. He has clearly short, sturdy dwarf proportions, a short beard already streaked with gray, observant eyes, and patched practical travel clothes. His expression is eloquent but quietly tired and guarded, without a broad smile. He carries a rustic rabeca (a small handmade bowed fiddle) and its bow close to his torso; show this as a traveling musician's instrument, not a guitar. Three-quarter view from head to upper legs, comfortable margins, both hands anatomically clear. Plain light background with no scenery or ground shadow.
Canonical constraints: his half-medallion is hidden beneath clothing and must not be visible. No hammer, axe, armor, crown, visible magic, water powers, fangs or heroic combat pose. He is distinct from the reference dwarf: shorter beard, younger face, travel clothes and fiddle, no copied Gorvak identity or equipment.
Output: PNG; clean silhouette suitable for a visual-novel dialogue portrait. Use a genuinely transparent exterior if supported; otherwise a uniform pure white exterior.
```
