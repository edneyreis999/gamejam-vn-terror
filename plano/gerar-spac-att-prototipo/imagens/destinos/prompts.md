## Caminho da Igreja

Arquivo: `caminho-da-igreja.png`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: landscape destination-selection illustration for the offline horror visual novel Afogados em Terra Seca.
Input image 1: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, the supplied drawing of the dwarf Gorvak. Create a new environment illustration; do not include Gorvak or another character.
Primary request: depict the Caminho da Igreja, a neglected rural path leading to a small old church in a fantastical riverside region inspired by inland Paraná, Brazil. A winding dirt path, rough stone foundations, weathered plaster walls, an old tiled roof and modest bell tower, surrounded by tangled vegetation and a few araucaria silhouettes. The church exterior is the clear visual landmark. Use the environment to suggest silence and unease.
Style/medium: closely match the attached Gorvak's confident hand-drawn black ink contours, varied line weight, fine irregular hatching and loose sketch marks. Flat warm pale beige fills with off-white/white areas; shadow created by black hatching. This should look drawn by the same artist as the hero, at a similar finish level. No painterly rendering, gradients, glossy lighting, 3D or photorealism.
Composition: one coherent wide environmental scene, landscape 3:2 framing. Strong readable silhouette and a simple hierarchy that remains clear as a small destination card. Keep the main landmark comfortably inside the frame.
Constraints: late-nineteenth-century equivalent fantasy setting without humans. No people or creatures, no visible imprisoned character, no plot revelations, no medallion, no map, no modern technology. Ordinary church architecture, no inverted religious symbols, no Halloween decoration. No text, labels, captions, border, interface, logo or watermark.
```
